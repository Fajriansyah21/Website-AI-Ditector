## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-precision forensic instrument tailored for deep linguistic inspection, statistical perplexity scanning, and metadata purification. The visual identity bridges the clinical rigor of an analytical laboratory with the measured typographic balance of a modern investigative editorial suite.

### Target Audience & Emotional Stance
Designed for Indonesian legal analysts, academic reviewers, editorial gatekeepers, and intelligence researchers. The interface projects absolute authority, scientific objectivity, and unhurried scrutiny. It rejects noisy synthetic gradients and toy-like generative AI aesthetics, favoring instead calibrated density, crisp structural divisions, and surgical data clarity.

### Aesthetic Paradigm: Forensic Laboratory
- **Structure & Demarcation:** Precision wireframe-informed architecture utilizing calibrated 1px borders, subtle surface offsets, and restrained translucent overlays.
- **Surface Depth:** Subdued slate-indigo foundations paired with micro-milled glass containers that focus attention directly on linguistic anomalies.
- **Diagnostic Signaling:** Tri-state probabilistic color codings (Emerald for human authenticity, Crimson for synthetic artifacts, Amber for burstiness/entropy variance) engineered strictly for rapid scanning without cognitive fatigue.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around an ocular-sparing dark canvas, simulating an illuminated laboratory console. Pure black (`#000000`) is avoided to eliminate high-contrast halation during long forensic sessions; the baseline environment utilizes deep midnight indigo-tinted obsidian.

### Functional Roles & Spectrum
- **Canvas Base (`#090D16`):** The foundational substrate for all workspace views.
- **Surface Elevation (`#0E1526` to `#162038`):** Deep charcoal-indigo surfaces providing tonal containment.
- **Primary / Brand Accent (`#6366F1` & `#818CF8`):** Deep indigo and radiant violet accents representing computational intelligence and analytical operations.
- **Diagnostic Status - Authentic / Human (`#10B981` & `#34D399`):** Crisp emerald/teal utilized for high human confidence ratings, verified document provenance, and stripped metadata verification.
- **Diagnostic Status - Synthetic / AI Flagged (`#F43F5E` & `#FB7185`):** Rose-crimson reserved for synthetically flagged phrases, uniform token distributions, and exposed document fingerprint anomalies.
- **Diagnostic Status - Anomalous / Perplexity Shift (`#F59E0B` & `#FBBF24`):** Amber tone indicating statistical uncertainty, mixed human-machine revision, or uneven lexical bursts.
- **Borders & Dividers (`#1E293B` / `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`):** Hairline precision markers maintaining structural containment across dark tiers.

## Typography

The typographic engine balances crisp editorial readability with technical data representation. 

### Font Selection & Roles
- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Headings & Body):** Employs geometric clarity, open apertures, and excellent counter-form readability. Ideal for Indonesian prose analysis where compound formal terms (*mempertanggungjawabkannya*, *keberlanjutannya*) require distinct letter spacing and unhindered legibility.
- **JetBrains Mono (Telemetry, Metadata & Metrics):** Provides non-proportional tabular clarity for score calculations, perplexity indexes, token probabilities, timestamp logs, and stripped binary metadata signatures.

### Reading Rules
All analysis viewports displaying analyzed Indonesian copy must use `body-lg` or `body-md` with `lineHeight` locked to no less than 1.6x the font size to allow diagnostic highlight overlays (sentence background tints) to coexist without overlapping descenders or ascenders.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is anchored by an asymmetric workspace designed for split-screen comparison: the forensic input/document feed on the left, and real-time telemetry/metadata inspectors on the right.

### Grid & Layout Structure
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12-column analytical grid with a split-console structure. The main reading console occupies 7 or 8 columns, while the diagnostic telemetry panel occupies the remaining 4 or 5 columns. Fixed 2rem outer canvas margins with 1.25rem internal column gutters.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout. The inspector cascades into an expandable drawer or bottom-docked sheet with 1.5rem canvas margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with 1rem canvas margins. Views flip between raw text editor mode and diagnostic report tabs.

### Spacing Cadence
Component internals use compact steps: `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px) for badge internals, score chips, and dense metadata tables; `space-md` (16px) for module padding; and `space-lg` (24px) for distinct card separations.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy drop shadows, relying instead on structural wireframes, surface luminescence, and frosted backdrop blurs that convey a sterile, high-tech instrument environment.

### Depth Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Solid `#090D16`. Zero elevation. Housing background grids and analytical canvas guides.
- **Level 1 (Panels & Substrates):** Solid `#0E1526` framed by a 1px solid border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`. Houses document editors and analytical toolbars.
- **Level 2 (Diagnostic Cards & Meters):** Translucent `#162038` backed by an 8px blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`), bounded by `rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.15)` border on hover or active state.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays & Inspector Popovers):** Solid `#121A2F` with a diffuse peripheral glow: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65), 0 0 0 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`.
- **Level 4 (Forensic Tooltips):** Pure `#1E293B` floating chips with crisp 1px borders colored to match the associated diagnostic state (Emerald, Crimson, or Amber).

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a disciplined, technical baseline (`roundedness: 1` — Soft, base 4px). Rounded forms are kept compact to maintain an architectural, instrument-like silhouette rather than a consumer app aesthetic.

### Radius Specifications
- **Base (4px):** Form fields, action buttons, diagnostic badge tags, score indicators, and table cell selections.
- **Large (8px):** Diagnostic cards, inspector sidebars, code/metadata clean preview viewports, and modal dialogs.
- **Extra Large (12px):** Root application boundaries and dedicated drag-and-drop document upload zones.
- **Pill (Fully Rounded):** Restricted exclusively to forensic status pills (e.g., `98.4% MANUSIA`) and micro state flags to distinguish contextual indicators from interactive controls.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Forensic Action (e.g., "Mulai Analisis", "Bersihkan Metadata"):** Indigo gradient foundation (`#6366F1` to `#4F46E5`), 4px border radius, high-contrast white typography (`label-lg`), subtle inner border highlight (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,0.2)`).
- **Secondary / Action Surface:** Transparent substrate, 1px border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, hover illumination `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`.
- **Destructive / Forensic Reset:** Crimson outline (`rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.3)`) with subdued text shifting to solid crimson fill on deliberate engagement.

### Diagnostic Chips & Badge Tags
- Built using `JetBrains Mono` at `label-sm` or `label-md`. Height 22px.
- **Human Authenticity Badge:** Translucent teal background (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.1)`), emerald text (`#34D399`), 1px border (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.25)`), paired with a solid 4px emerald indicator dot.
- **AI Highlight Badge:** Translucent crimson background (`rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.1)`), rose text (`#FB7185`), 1px border (`rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.25)`).
- **Entropy / Uncertainty Badge:** Translucent amber background (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.1)`), warm yellow text (`#FBBF24`), 1px border (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.25)`).

### Forensic Text Highlighting Engine
- Analyzed Indonesian prose within the document viewport is segmented at the clause/sentence boundary.
- **AI-Identified Spans:** Background `rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.14)`, bottom border marker `1.5px solid #F43F5E`. Hovering reveals a precision popover displaying perplexity score, burstiness value, and one-click "Humanize" refactoring options.
- **Mixed / Edited Spans:** Background `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.14)`, bottom border marker `1.5px solid #F59E0B`.
- **Verified Human Spans:** Default text color without intrusive background wash, shifting to subtle emerald wash (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.08)`) during layer isolation mode.

### Precision Technical Meters & Gauges
- **Radial Forensics Dial:** Dual-track circular gauge displaying total authenticity percentage (0-100%). Background track `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`, active track dynamic transition between Crimson (`< 40%`), Amber (`40-75%`), and Emerald (`> 75%`). Monospaced metric centered in `headline-md`.
- **Perplexity & Burstiness Linear Sparklines:** 32px height bar-series charts showing paragraph-by-paragraph statistical uniformity. Flat sequences indicate synthetic text generation, jagged peaks indicate human cadence.

### Metadata Cleaner & Inspection Panels
- **Key-Value Ledger:** Two-column table with alternating rows (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.01)` and transparent). Monospaced field keys (e.g., `Author`, `Producer`, `GPS_Coords`, `CreateDate`) styled in muted slate.
- **Purification Toggle Bar:** Individual strike-through indicators showing data slated for binary erasure. Purged items flagged with a teal `CLEANSED` tag.

### Input Fields & Workspace Editors
- **Text Lab Canvas:** Frameless or hairline-bounded text fields with transparent background, syntax-level focus state signaled by a 1px perimeter glow in Indigo (`rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.4)`). Placeholder styled in muted slate (`#475569`).

### Checkboxes & Segmented Controls
- **Segmented Forensic Toggles (e.g., "Semua Lapisan | Hanya AI | Metadata"):** Enclosed 4px container, dark surface `#0E1526`, active state sliding slate-indigo tab with crisp 1px border.
- **Checkboxes:** 14x14px square, 2px border radius, sharp monochrome check glyph when active, accented with emerald for metadata retention controls.
